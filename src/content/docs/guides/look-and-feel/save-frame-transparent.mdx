---
title: "Save Frame (Transparent)"
---

## How to Use

1. Open your project  
2. Move the **playhead** to the frame you want  
3. Menu bar → **Frame → Save Frame (Transparent)**  
4. Choose a location and save  

---

## What You Get

- Image with **transparent background (alpha channel)**  
- Exported as a file (e.g. PNG)  
- Full resolution of your video  

---

## Use Cases

- Create marketing assets  
- Place UI into device mockups  
- Export clean visuals for design work  
- Keep reusable assets outside the editor

## Tip

For best results:
- Keep the subject **well-isolated**  
- Use **zoom or lightbox** before exporting  
- Avoid cluttered frames → transparency will expose it
