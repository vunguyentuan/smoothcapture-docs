## Save Current Frame

Save any frame from your recording as an **image file** on your Mac.

---

## How to Use

1. Open your project
2. Move the **playhead** to the frame you want
3. On menu bar → **Frame → Save Current Frame** or usining shortcur **Command-Shift-S**
4. Choose a location and save

---

<video controls className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="/videos/save-frame.mp4" />

## Output

- Exported as an image file (e.g. PNG)
- Full resolution of your video

---

## When to Use

- Create thumbnails
- Save assets for design or marketing
- Keep a reference snapshot outside the editor

---

## Tip

Don’t just save random frames - pick moments that are **sharp, centered, and intentional**.
