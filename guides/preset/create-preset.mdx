## What is a Preset?

A preset saves your **editing setup** so you can reuse it across recordings.

It can include things like:
- Background
- Aspect ratio
- Device Frame
- webcame overlay 

---

## Why Use Presets

If you’re creating multiple videos (tutorials, demos, series), presets help you:
- Stay **visually consistent**  
- Skip repetitive setup  
- Move faster from recording → export  

---

## Create a Preset

1. Open a project or recording  
2. Adjust settings to your desired style  
3. Click **Presets**  
4. Select **Create New Preset**  
5. Give it a name and save

## Apply a Preset

- Open a new recording  
- Choose your preset from the **Presets** menu  
- All saved settings will be applied instantly  

---

## Tip

Don’t create too many presets.

Most people only need:
- 1 for tutorials  
- 1 for social content  
- 1 for marketing  

Anything more = clutter, not productivity.
