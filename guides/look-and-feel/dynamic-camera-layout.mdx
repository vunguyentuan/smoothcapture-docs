## Dynamic Camera Layouts

Dynamic Camera Layouts let you control how your webcam appears throughout your recording - fullscreen, side-by-side, overlay or hidden.

Instead of keeping the camera static, you can change its layout at different moments to match your content.

---

## Create a Camera Layout
Open the **Camera Layouts** track and insert a new layout segment where needed.

Once added, layouts behave like other timeline segments — you can move, trim, or duplicate them.

---

## Edit a Camera Layout

Each layout defines how the camera appears during that segment:
- **Overlay**  
  Shows both the screen and camera. You can move and resize the camera freely.


- **Fullscreen**  
  The camera fills the entire frame.

- **Side-by-side**
  Shows both the screen and camera. You can move and resize the camera freely.

- **Hidden**  
  The camera is not shown.

Switching to **Default** allows full control — drag and position the camera anywhere on the screen.

---

## When to Use It

- **Fullscreen**: speaking directly to the audience
- **Side-by-side**: demos and walkthroughs
- **Overlay**: keeping your face visible without giving up screen space
- **Hidden**: focusing entirely on screen content
