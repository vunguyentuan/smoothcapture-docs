## Add Background Music

Enhance your recording with music to make it feel more engaging and polished.
Open the **Audio** panel on the right side of the editor to get started.

---

## Built-in Music

- Choose from a curated **royalty-free library**  
- Applies across your entire timeline  
- Safe to use for demos, tutorials, and commercial content  

---

## Add Your Own Music

1. Click **Add Audio**  
2. Upload your file (**MP3 or MP4**)  
3. The track will be added to your project  

---

## Tips

- Keep volume **low** → your voice or UI sounds should stay clear  
- Avoid distracting or overly intense tracks  
- Match the tone of your video (demo ≠ cinematic trailer)
