## How to Use

1. Open your project  
2. Move the **playhead** to the frame you want  
3. Menu bar → **Frame → Copy Frame (Transparent)**  
   or press **⌥ + ⌘ + ⇧ + C**  
4. Paste anywhere with **⌘ + V**

---

## What You Get

- Image with **transparent background (alpha channel)**  
- Ready for design tools, slides, or overlays  

---

## Use Cases

- Place UI into device mockups (MacBook, iPhone, etc.)  
- Create clean marketing visuals  
- Share UI snippets without background noise  
- Add polished visuals to docs or social posts

## Tip

For best results:
- Keep the subject **well-isolated**  
- Use **zoom or lightbox** to guide focus  
- Avoid cluttered frames → transparency will expose it
