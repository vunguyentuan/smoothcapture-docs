## Disable Automatic Zoom

By default, SmoothCapture can create zooms automatically based on your interactions.

If you prefer full control, you can turn this off and use **Manual Zoom** instead.

---

## How to Disable

1. Open **Settings**  
2. Go to the **General** tab  
3. Turn off **Auto-generate zoom effect**

---

![SCR 20260105 Oiyp](/images/disable-auto-zoom.png)

## When to Disable

- You want **precise control** over every zoom  
- Your recording has **few or no clicks**  
- You’re aiming for a more **intentional, cinematic style**

---

## Tip

Automatic zoom is fast, but not always accurate.

If your video feels random or unfocused,  
turn it off and control the zooms yourself.
